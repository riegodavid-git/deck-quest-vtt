Deck Quest – Game Overview

**Official game page & card art source:** https://www.garagesofagames.com/games/deck-quest

1. What is Deck Quest?
Deck Quest is a tabletop role-playing game (RPG) designed to be open-ended, flexible, and highly adaptable. Unlike rigid systems, it thrives on creativity and player-driven storytelling.

Open-Ended RPG: The rules are intentionally loose, allowing groups to tailor the experience to their preferences.

Role of the Game Master (GM): One player acts as the GM, guiding the adventure. The GM narrates the story, manages non-player characters (NPCs) and adversaries, interprets player actions, and designs quests using the Deck Quest guidebook.

2. Basic Rules
Setup: The GM prepares a quest framework. Each player is dealt five cards, ideally consisting of one class card and four attribute cards.

Character Creation: Players select their class and attributes. Racial attributes are particularly valuable due to their strong bonuses. For example:

A Dwarven Paladin might emphasize racial attributes for high health.

A Wizard might choose Talented to gain extra roll points.

This mix of class and attributes defines each character’s strengths, weaknesses, and playstyle.

3. Core Gameplay Loop
Location Management  
The GM draws location cards, which act as the game map. Each card depicts specific areas (e.g., chambers in a sunken dungeon) that guide exploration.

Player Choices  
Players decide where to go and what actions to take. Their decisions shape the narrative and determine the challenges they encounter.

Adversaries & Combat  
When danger arises, the GM introduces adversaries (e.g., an undead warrior). Players declare attacks, and the GM rolls to resolve outcomes. Adversaries have unique abilities such as:

Dead Chill (unblockable)

Fearless Strike (high-risk, high-reward)

Rewards & Progression  
Victories yield item cards that enhance characters. For example, an Iron Long Sword grants +4 to combat rolls, boosting effectiveness in battle.

Quest Completion  
The party travels between locations (e.g., from a sunken dungeon to a blazing desert) until the quest’s objective is achieved.

Open-Ended Play  
The GM can extend the story indefinitely or create new quests, ensuring replayability and long-term engagement.

4. Combat and Progression in Detail
Adversaries: Each enemy has defined actions and traits. The GM controls them, creating tactical challenges.

Combat Resolution: Players describe their attacks, and dice rolls determine success, failure, and damage.

Rewards: Defeated enemies may drop items. These items provide stat modifiers, durability limits, or special effects, encouraging strategic use.

5. Completing the Quest
Travel: The party moves across diverse environments, each location card expanding the adventure.

Winning: A quest ends when the party fulfills the GM’s defined objective. However, since Deck Quest is open-ended, the GM can seamlessly continue the story or launch new quests, keeping the game alive as long as players remain engaged.

Summary
Deck Quest is a story-driven, card-based RPG that balances structure with freedom. The GM sets the stage, but player choices drive the narrative. With its mix of location exploration, adversary encounters, item rewards, and flexible quest design, Deck Quest offers a dynamic and endlessly replayable tabletop experience.
